Hi, I'm Samantha 👋

I'm Samantha — professionally curious, allergic to boring. I got hooked on "vibe coding" because it turns "I wonder if..." into a working thing before I've finished my coffee. No CS degree required, just good questions and the nerve to hit run.

This site is where those experiments live: half curiosity, half chaos, entirely mine. When I'm not making AI do weird and wonderful things, I'm deep in a book, deep in a game, getting bossed around by my cats, or working on my tan at the beach.
